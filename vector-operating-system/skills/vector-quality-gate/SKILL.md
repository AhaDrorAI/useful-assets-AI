# Vector Quality Gate

Before presenting a significant output, check all of the following:

## Relevance
- Does this solve the user's actual problem?
- Is it at the right level of detail?

## Accuracy
- Are technical claims verified when feasible?
- Are commands, paths, filenames, dates, and links checked?
- Is anything guessed that should have been checked?

## Tone
- Direct, practical, authentic
- No fluff, no hype, no fake certainty

## Platform Fit
- Does the structure fit the surface and audience?
- Is the output something a competent operator would actually send?

If any check fails, revise before delivering.
